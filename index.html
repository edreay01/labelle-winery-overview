Recap of the technical walkthrough

Platform & Capabilities

Your current stack — WordPress + WooCommerce, a wall of plugins, dual UPS keys, and Zapier automations — maps onto Shopify Plus almost entirely with native features. Two things need paid apps: wine compliance/shipping and events ticketing. Those two are the ~$5K of third-party apps in the cost model below.

🍷
Paid app

Wine compliance & shipping

Vino Shipper via a native connector (Crush Suite / Whirlwind) — checkout, branding and data stay in Shopify. Handles NH/MA/FL licensing and DOB verification.

🎟️
Paid app

Events ticketing

Your #1 online revenue. A purpose-built app (Evey / GM Ticketing) replaces FooEvents with customer self-service — cancels, transfers, credits, dietary notes.

📦
Native

Dual UPS & mixed cart

Shipping profiles apply wine rates to wine and standard rates to everything else — in one cart. Removes the reason for two separate stores.

🔁
Native

Wine & Vineyard Clubs

Automated recurring shipments and a self-serve member portal replace Ashlynn's manual Zapier process — with room for the Winemaker's Kitchen culinary club.

🖥️
Native

CMS & compliance reporting

Built-in CMS for a phased WordPress migration; Shopify Flow auto-sends Williams Compliance reports on a schedule.

🚀
Native

Room to grow

Open APIs to bridge future hotel/spa systems and Triple Seat — consolidating a fragmented stack onto one platform.

The two paid apps = the ~$5K app line

Everything else you pay for today — hosting, CDN, security, fraud tooling, and most plugins — is included natively in Shopify Plus. Only wine compliance and events carry a dedicated app cost.

Naming the cost up front

On the platform itself, Shopify Plus plus those two apps runs about $21,500 a year more than your current website stack. That's the real increase, and it's worth being clear about it. It's also the smaller number on the page — the far larger figure is what you currently pay to accept a card, which is where Section 2 goes next.

Source: discovery calls Jul 13–27, 2026; Use-Case Deep Dive.
